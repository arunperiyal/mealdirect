About MealDirect

The kitchens around you, one tap away.

Every neighbourhood has them: the mess that's been feeding students and office workers for years, the home kitchen whose lunch everyone talks about. Their food is fresh, honest and fairly priced, but ordering it usually means a phone call, a WhatsApp message or a walk down the road.

MealDirect puts those kitchens in an app. Kitchens publish the menu they're cooking for each meal; you order ahead and choose when it arrives. Because orders come in before cooking starts, kitchens cook what's needed and waste less, and you get a hot meal at the time you asked for.

A rider from your area brings it to your door, and you pay them there, in cash or by UPI. No cards, no wallets to top up.

What we care about

The ideas behind how MealDirect works.

Fresh, not fast

Menus are cooked for the orders placed, not kept warm for hours. Ordering ahead is what makes that possible.

Fair to small kitchens

Tools that fit how messes actually work: menus per meal, ordering times that run overnight, and a count of what to cook.

Local all the way

Kitchens and riders from your own area, rated by the people they feed.
