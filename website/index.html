How it works

Kitchens cook one menu in bulk for each meal, so you order ahead and your food comes fresh at the time you choose.

Pick a kitchen and a menu

See what's cooking today and tomorrow. A kitchen can have several menus a day: breakfast, lunch, dinner.

Choose a time and an address

Pick a delivery slot, or collect it yourself. Save your home and office addresses: lunch can go to one, dinner to the other.

Pay when it arrives

Hand cash to your delivery partner or scan their UPI code. Then rate the food and the delivery.

Run your kitchen on MealDirect

The MealDirect Partner app is built for messes and kitchens that cook to order, every day.

  • Publish a menu for each meal, with your own ordering times, even overnight
  • The kitchen view adds up how much of each dish to cook, per delivery time
  • Accept or mark a whole delivery slot ready in one tap
  • Customer ratings and order statements to download
Get MealDirect Partner

Deliver with MealDirect

Pick up meals from local kitchens and deliver them to your neighbourhood, on your own schedule.

  • Claim orders from a shared queue, or set auto-accept for kitchens you know
  • Show the customer a UPI QR code at the door
  • See your cash to settle, your ratings and your delivery statements
