How to use MealDirect

There's an app for each person in the chain: the customer who orders, the kitchen that cooks, the rider who delivers, and the MealDirect team. Each one works on Android or in the browser, with the same account.

CustomersKitchensRidersMealDirect teamQuestions
MealDirect app

Order a meal

Order today's or tomorrow's menu ahead of time, and have it delivered when you want it.

Android app Open in browser
  1. Sign upEnter your name, email and a password. We email you a 6-digit code to confirm the address.
  2. Pick a kitchenBrowse the messes and kitchens near you. A kitchen's page shows all its menus for the day, one after the other: breakfast, lunch, dinner.
  3. Fill your cartAdd dishes from one menu. You can switch to another menu of the same kitchen without emptying your cart first.
  4. Choose a time and an addressPick a delivery slot, or collect it yourself. Save your home and office addresses once, and choose one for each menu at checkout.
  5. Pay at the doorPay your rider in cash, or scan the UPI code they show you. Follow the order from the kitchen to your door in the app.
  6. Rate itOnce it arrives, rate the food and the delivery. Kitchens and riders see what you think.
MealDirect Partner

Run your kitchen

Publish your menus, take orders until the time you choose, and see exactly how much to cook.

Android app Open in browser
  1. Sign up and set up your kitchenAdd your kitchen's name, address and payout details. The MealDirect team checks them before your kitchen goes live.
  2. Add your dishesKeep a list of the dishes you make, with prices, so building a menu takes a minute.
  3. Publish a menu for each mealName each menu, such as Lunch or Dinner, and set when ordering opens and closes. Ordering can run overnight, for example from 8 pm to 9 am. Delete drafts you no longer need.
  4. Cook to the kitchen viewThe kitchen view adds up how much of each dish to cook for each delivery time.
  5. Accept and hand overAccept orders, or a whole delivery slot at once, and mark them ready for the rider.
  6. Follow upRead your ratings and download order statements for any period as a CSV file.
MealDirect Delivery

Deliver orders

Pick up meals from local kitchens and bring them to people nearby, when it suits you.

Android app Open in browser
  1. Sign upAdd your details. The MealDirect team approves riders before they can take orders.
  2. Claim ordersTake orders from the shared queue as kitchens mark them ready.
  3. Or let them come to youSet auto-accept for kitchens you know, for a time window, and choose how many orders you take at once.
  4. Deliver and collectPick up from the kitchen, deliver, and collect cash or show the customer your UPI code.
  5. Keep trackSee the cash you have to settle, your ratings, and download your delivery statements.
MealDirect Admin

Look after the platform

For the MealDirect team only: accounts are created by the team, not by signing up.

Android app Open in browser
  1. Approve kitchens and ridersCheck new kitchens and riders before they go live, and suspend them if needed.
  2. Review changesApprove or reject changes to payout and personal details.
  3. Watch ordersFollow every order across kitchens and riders.
  4. Manage accountsDelete an account on request, or restore one deleted by mistake.

Questions

Can't find the answer? Send us a message with the form at the bottom of the page.

How do I install the Android app?

Download it from the download page and open the file. Your phone may ask you to allow installs from your browser first; the download page has the steps.

I forgot my password.

Tap "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen. We email you a 6-digit code, and you choose a new password. You're signed out everywhere else.

Do I need to pay online?

No. You pay when your food arrives, in cash or by UPI.

Can I order for lunch and dinner at once?

Each order is for one menu. Place one order for lunch and another for dinner; each can go to a different address.

How do I get my order history?

The customer, partner and delivery apps each have a statement page: choose a period and download your orders as a CSV file, which opens in any spreadsheet.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Profile tab (Settings for kitchens, Account for riders) and choose Delete account, then confirm with your password. If you change your mind, write to us and we can restore it.
